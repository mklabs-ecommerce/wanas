# System prompt audit — 2026-10-02

Line-by-line review of the production system prompt (`docs/system_prompt_full.md`
as of 1d7ee38), traced back to its sources and fixed there. The dump is
regenerated by `python scripts/rehla/dump_system_prompt.py`.

**Size:** 24,473 → 21,197 characters (−13%). Production logs show 18–21k
prompt tokens per model call, most of it this prompt, so the cut is paid back
on every hop. The test ceiling moved from 24,500 to 22,000 so the room that
freed is not quietly spent again.

**Order:** the anti-hallucination rules ("from the tools only") moved from the
middle to the top, directly under the identity line; tone and formatting come
next; flows (discovery, purchase, checkout, sizes, photos, after the order,
returns, handoff) follow.

## Verification

- `pytest`: 2462 passed, 22 skipped. `ruff check .`: clean.
- `python scripts/rehla/demo_conversations.py --fresh`, real model (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flash` via OpenRouter), 12 scenarios. The final run passed all 12. Scenarios added: «اه» 18 hours after a pending offer, a male customer buying a gift (محمد), an unavailable category (فساتين سواريه), a return for a delivered order, a greeting with no category menu, role-change + off-topic, a complaint.
- Over the iterations the demo found and fixed: the handoff promised with no call, the exchange-vs-refund loop, the complaint asking for an order number, the «آسفة» self-reference, the instruction echoed back, the free-exchange claim, «أأكد الأوردر؟» without the summary, the late «اه» re-asking, and the scope redirect being refused as a repeat.
- Still model-dependent (prompt plus a check, but GLM is not deterministic): the order of checkout questions (address and governorate sometimes swap) and occasional odd phrasing («تسجيل يا منة»). The `reply_rules` dialect checks catch the known bad words, not every awkward sentence.

## Open questions — yours to decide

1. **Rehla Jacket price.** Live Shopify: 1000 (compare-at 1200). The seed is now 1000. Is 1000 right? Production Postgres was seeded with 100 and the seed doesn't reach an existing DB, so if Shopify is ever unreachable the bot quotes 100 until you fix it in the dashboard (Products → Rehla Jacket).
2. **Who pays shipping on a size exchange?** The code (`shop_facts.return_eligibility`) and now the prompt say the customer pays unless it's a manufacturing defect. Your brief only says "deductions". If size exchanges should be free, that's one line in `return_eligibility` plus the prompt sentence.
3. **Default gender.** Now: neutral first, and if a sentence can't be neutral, feminine. Do you want feminine as the outright default instead (most customers are women), or keep neutral-first? Your WhatsApp templates in `REHLA.md` (`rehla_feedback_request`, `rehla_back_in_stock`) are feminine-only («تقيّمي», «كنتي مستنياها») and will reach the men who order too.
4. **Online payment on the website.** The Wanas-era wording said «أونلاين من الموقع» is allowed. I removed it: the bot takes COD only. If Rehla's Shopify checkout does take cards and you want the bot to mention it for website orders, say so and it becomes a published fact in `shop_facts`.
5. **Hoodies and caps are all sold out live** (inventory 0 on both hoodies and both caps). The bot won't offer them, but they're still ACTIVE in Shopify. Archive them, or restock?
6. **Size charts.** 16 of 21 Shopify products carry `custom.size_chart_data`. Yoga pants, both hoodies and both caps don't, so a sizing question on those goes to a person (`size_help`). Add charts for them?
7. **Railway service name.** Production runs as the `wanas` service from `mklabs-ecommerce/wanas`. It works; renaming is cosmetic and your call.
