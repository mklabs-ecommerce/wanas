# Exchange, Refund & Cancellation Policy

The shop's own wording, kept here because `FEATURES.md` §1.9 describes what the
bot is allowed to say about it and had nothing in the repo to point at. This
file is the source of truth for the terms; nothing loads it at runtime.

## Refunds & Returns
Packages are allowed to be opened at the door, so you can check them properly before accepting. If there is any issue with your order, you can give it back to the courier on the spot and you will only be charged the shipping fees.

Returns are only accepted through the courier at the time of delivery.

## Exchanges
If you would like to request an exchange, please contact us after receiving the package. We will ensure that your exchange is processed as quickly as possible.

- Exchange requests must be initiated within 24 hours of receiving your order.
- The item must remain in its original packaging, unworn, and clean.
- If the item is defective or you received the wrong item, the shipping fees for the exchange will be covered by the company.
- If you are requesting an exchange due to personal preference (size, color, etc.), you will be responsible for the exchange delivery fees.
- An extra 20 EGP is charged for exchange shipping, in addition to the regular shipping fees.

## Order Cancellation
- You can cancel your order free of charge at any time **before it has been shipped**. Just contact us and we'll process the cancellation and refund right away.
- Once the order has been shipped (handed to the courier), it can no longer be cancelled remotely. If you refuse the package at the door, this will be treated as a return, and you will be responsible for the round-trip shipping fees (both the delivery attempt and the return trip).
- To request a cancellation, simply contact us the same way you would request an exchange (WhatsApp / phone / whichever channel we use).
